The three problems

parser: dates parse month first. 379 reports from 11 tenants. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so any date with a day of 12 or less comes out swapped: 7 April becomes 4 July. This is the bug #4471 names. Tracked in issue 1.

hint: the booking-v3 form tells people to type month first. 33 reports, all through the form on the 3 booking-v3 tenants. The form prints MM/DD/YYYY, people follow it, and the form reads their date day first. The symptom looks like the parser bug, but the parser fix will not touch it. Tracked in issue 2.

shift: the date moves by one day. 5 reports. Nothing is swapped: 12 May shows as 11 May, a meeting lands on Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto, an invite arrives before the booking. That is a time-zone or daylight-saving conversion. The desk had already marked these as a one-day move in its notes, but this kb only footnoted them under parser, so they never got a ticket. In the desk’s view they stay counted under parser, where their tenant files them, and the footnote under the table points here. Tracked in issue 3.

Why they were merged

The desk merges on subject line (decision 0001), and all three problems arrive with the same subjects: “Date is wrong”, “Wrong day on the invite”. Intake tags did not help either: the tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser, and it disagrees with the tenant’s form on dozens of the reports in the table above. The cause comes from the tenant and the way the report came in, and the desk’s own reading of the body: src/lib/kb/causes.ts.

  • npm run counts runs the same causes.ts this page runs and prints the reports per cause, and how many of them the desk read as a one-day move.
