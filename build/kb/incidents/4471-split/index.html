The two problems

parser: dates parse month first. 379 reports from 11 tenants. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so any date with a day of 12 or less comes out swapped: 7 April becomes 4 July. This is the bug #4471 names. Fixed: the parser now reads dd/mm for EU locales. Issue 1, closed.

hint: the booking form tells people to type month first. 33 reports, all through the form on the 3 booking-v3 tenants. The form prints MM/DD/YYYY, people follow it, and the form reads their date day first. The symptom looks like the parser bug, and the parser fix did not touch it. Won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired, so no new reports can come in this way. Issue 2, closed.

The desk read 5 of the reports as a one-day move rather than a swap (the footnote under the table). They come from day-first tenants outside the booking form, so they are filed under parser and counted there, not as a third problem.

Why they were merged

The desk merges on subject line (decision 0001), and both problems arrive with the same subjects: “Date is wrong”, “Wrong day on the invite”. Intake tags did not help either: the tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser, and it disagrees with the tenant’s form on dozens of the reports in the table above. The cause comes from the tenant and the way the report came in: src/lib/kb/causes.ts.
